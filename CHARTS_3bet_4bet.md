# 3-Bet / 4-Bet Charts — 6-max NL50/NL100 (100bb, rake-adjusted)

> **YOUR #2 LEAK.** You 3-bet 19.7% when facing a raise — that's ~2x too high.
> Target overall 3-bet ~**9–11%**. The hands to CUT: offsuit medium broadways,
> small/medium pairs as 3-bets, and all cold-4-bets with non-premium hands.
> Structure = **polarized** (value + suited blocker-bluffs), NOT a merge of
> medium offsuit junk.

Size guide (3-bet):
- **IP** (e.g. BTN vs CO open): **3x** the open (~7.5bb vs a 2.5bb open)
- **OOP** (blinds vs open, or SB vs BTN): **4x** the open (~10bb)
- Bigger when deeper / vs limpers.

## 3-BET vs an OPEN — by your position vs theirs

### You in the BLINDS vs a steal (BTN/CO open) — most common spot
```
VALUE:   TT+, AQs+, AKo, AJs, KQs
BLUFF:   A5s, A4s, A3s, A2s (wheel aces = top blockers)
         KJs, QJs, JTs, T9s, 98s (suited, mix — not every time)
```
Fold the rest. **Do NOT 3-bet offsuit AJ/KQ/AT or small pairs from the blinds** —
flat the playable ones (see BB/SB charts), fold the junk.

### You in the BLINDS vs an EP/MP open (tighter)
```
VALUE:   QQ+, AKs, AKo, AQs
BLUFF:   A5s, A4s only
```

### You on the BTN vs a CO/MP open (in position)
```
VALUE:   99+, AQo+, AJs+, KQs
BLUFF:   A5s-A2s, KJs, QTs mix, suited connectors occasionally
```

### You in CO vs an UTG/MP open (tight — they're repping strength)
```
VALUE:   TT+, AQs+, AKo
BLUFF:   A5s, A4s
```

## FACING A 4-BET (you 3-bet, villain 4-bets)  ← fixes your SB punt

**Default vs a 4-bet / all-in: continue ONLY with the top of your range.**
```
CALL/JAM:  QQ+, AKs, AKo   (vs a very aggressive 4-bettor add JJ, AQs)
5-BET JAM bluffs: A5s/A4s occasionally vs known aggro regs ONLY
FOLD:      everything else — including 55, AJ, KQ, A5s most of the time
```
> ⚠️ Reference hand #RC4860405039: you 3-bet 55 from the SB and called off vs a jam.
> 55 is **not** in the 3-bet range above from the SB, and even if it were, it is a
> SNAP-FOLD vs a 4-bet jam. This single fix saves ~$60/occurrence.

```
BLUFF:   A5s rarely, vs known-light 3-bettors only
FOLD:    AQ, AJ, KQ, TT, JJ (just call or fold) — and NEVER cold-4-bet ATo
```
> ⚠️ Reference hand #RC4860821070: you cold-4-bet ATo. That's a flat-or-fold, never

## 4-BETTING when YOU opened and face a 3-bet
```
VALUE 4-BET: QQ+, AKs, AKo
BLUFF 4-BET: A5s, A4s (blocker bluffs), occasionally KQs vs aggro 3-bettor
FLAT the 3-bet (IP, don't 4-bet): JJ, TT, AQs, AJs, KQs — realize equity
FOLD to the 3-bet: everything that was a thin open (small pairs, weak suited, offsuit broadways from EP)
```

### POPULATION ADJUSTMENT (NL50/NL100 GG Rush)
- Population **under-bluffs 4-bets** → when you get 4-bet, believe it. Fold more.
- Population **over-folds to 3-bets** when they open light from late position →
  this is where your *suited blocker bluffs* print. Keep those, cut the offsuit junk.
- Against sticky callers, your 3-bet **value** range can be bet relentlessly postflop;
  your 3-bet **bluffs** should mostly give up when called and they continue — don't
  pile in a second barrel with no equity (your recurring leak).
